Shipping & Delivery

1. Shipping Timelines

All orders are processed and shipped within 2-3 business days. Orders are not shipped or delivered on weekends or public holidays.

2. Delivery Estimates

Estimated delivery time for domestic orders is 5-7 business days. Delivery delays can occasionally occur depending on the courier partner or remote delivery locations.

3. Shipping Charges

Shipping charges for your order will be displayed at checkout.

4. Tracking Your Order

You will receive a shipment confirmation email or SMS containing your tracking number(s) once your order has shipped.

5. Contact Us

For tracking or delivery queries, please email us at hello@a-obd.in.
