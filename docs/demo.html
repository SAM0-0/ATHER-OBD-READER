What you are looking at

This is the A-OBD web dashboard running in a phone browser, a few seconds after the reader was plugged into the diagnostic port. Nothing is simulated — every value comes from the scooter’s own battery management system.

  • Battery health (SOH) — the percentage of original capacity your pack still holds. The Ather app shows charge and range, but not this number.
  • Per-cell voltages for each cell group in the pack, so you can see how evenly the battery is working instead of trusting a single average.
  • Cell imbalance — the gap between the strongest and weakest cell. A gap that widens between checks is the early warning of an ageing pack.
  • Charger data — input and output voltage and current while the scooter is charging.
  • Live charts that keep updating while you ride or charge, instead of a single frozen snapshot.
  • Extra values — controller temperature, throttle output, humidity, ambient temperature, PDU voltage and min/max cell SOH.

The video, in words

The recording is deliberately short and unglamorous: it shows what an owner actually sees. The reader is powered, the phone joins its Wi-Fi network, and the dashboard loads with live data in about ten seconds. From there the video scrolls through the main panels — battery health and pack summary first, then the individual cell readings, then charger telemetry while the scooter is plugged in.

What it deliberately does not show is a clean-room demo. This is the same interface you get at home: same Wi-Fi hotspot, same browser, same numbers coming off the CAN bus.

See this screen on your own scooter

1
Turn the key on and plug the reader in

The OBD port is only powered with the key on (or while charging). Open the boot, lift the cover and plug A-OBD into the diagnostic port, keeping the marked line on the connector facing up.

2
Power the reader and join its Wi-Fi

Connect it to a power bank or USB-C charger, then join the Ather-OBD network from your phone. The password is 12345678.

3
Open 192.168.4.1

Type http://192.168.4.1 into your phone browser (http, not https). The dashboard in the video is what loads — usually within about ten seconds.
