How to Use Ather-OBD

1
Turn the Key On

Turn the key on in your Ather scooter before doing anything else.

⚠️ Note: The OBD reader will NOT work if the key is off.

💡 If it's charging, no need to turn on the key.

2
Locate Port

In your boot, open the tiny cover to access your scooter's OBD port.

For 450 Series

For Rizta Series

3
Plug It In

Connect the Ather-OBD reader directly to the OBD port.

⚠️ Do NOT plug it in the wrong way! Make sure the marked line on the connector is on the top side, as shown in the images above. Be gentle with the connector, also if you connect it wrong way it will damage the pins inside connector and will damage it.

4
Power Up

Power the OBD reader using a power bank or any USB Type-C cable/charger.

🔴 Once powered, it will show a red LED. Make sure you see the red LED light up. If it doesn't, try a different cable or power source (power bank / charger).

5
Connect WiFi

Connect your phone to the WiFi network named Ather-OBD (Password: 12345678).

📶 After the red LED shows up, the WiFi normally takes about 5–10 seconds to appear. If it still doesn't show up after ~20 seconds, disconnect and reconnect the Type-C cable and try again.

6
View Live Data

Open exactly this link in your browser:

http://192.168.4.1

Wait about 10 seconds for the live telemetry data to appear.

⚠️ Do NOT use https:// — only use http:// exactly as shown above, otherwise no data will load!
