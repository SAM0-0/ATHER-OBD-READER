Check Your Ather's Real Battery Health at Home

The Ather app shows your charge and range, but not the number owners actually want: the battery's real State of Health (SOH). Service centres can read it over the scooter's CAN bus, but most owners are never shown the percentage. A-OBD changes that — this tiny plug & play OBD diagnostic tool connects to your scooter's diagnostic port and works as a battery health checker, showing your true SOH in minutes, without a service centre visit.

It goes beyond a single SOH number. A-OBD reads every cell in the pack — per-cell voltage, charge and temperatures — so you can spot cell imbalance early, the warning sign that shows up as uneven range drop long before a full failure. You also get charger input/output, motor temperature, odometer, drive modes and 40+ more live values, plus live charting and offline logging on the WROOM version.

Why does it matter? Ather's battery warranty guarantees the pack stays at or above 70% SOH during the cover period — knowing your real number helps you act early, back up a warranty claim, or value your scooter fairly on resale. It's 100% plug & play (no wires to cut), fully open source, and built by the community for Ather owners.

Every Ather model is supported — except Gen 1, Gen 1.5 and Gen 2. Whether you ride an Ather 450X, Ather 450S, Ather 450 Plus, Ather 450 Apex or an Ather Rizta Z, Rizta S or Rizta X — with the 2.9 kWh or the 3.7 kWh battery — the process is the same. Checking an Ather 450X battery health at home, verifying a Rizta SOH before buying second-hand, or hunting down a cell imbalance on a 450S all take three steps: plug in, connect, read.

How to Use A-OBD

1
Turn the Key On

Turn the key on in your scooter before doing anything else.

⚠️ Note: The OBD reader will NOT work if the key is off.

💡 If it's charging, no need to turn on the key.

2
Locate Port

In your boot, open the tiny cover to access your scooter's OBD port.

For 450 Series

For Rizta Series

3
Plug It In

Connect the A-OBD reader directly to the OBD port.

⚠️ Do NOT plug it in the wrong way! Make sure the marked line on the connector is on the top side, as shown in the images above. Be gentle with the connector, also if you connect it wrong way it will damage the pins inside connector and will damage it.

4
Power Up

Power the OBD reader using a power bank or any USB Type-C cable/charger.

🔴 Once powered, it will show a red LED. Make sure you see the red LED light up. If it doesn't, try a different cable or power source (power bank / charger).

5
Connect WiFi

Connect your phone to the WiFi network named Ather-OBD (Password: 12345678).

📶 After the red LED shows up, the WiFi normally takes about 5–10 seconds to appear. If it still doesn't show up after ~20 seconds, disconnect and reconnect the Type-C cable and try again.

6
View Live Data

Open exactly this link in your browser:

http://192.168.4.1

Wait about 10 seconds for the live telemetry data to appear.

⚠️ Do NOT use https:// — only use http:// exactly as shown above, otherwise no data will load!

⚠️ CRITICAL WARNINGS:

• Do NOT keep it connected while driving!

• Do NOT keep it connected for more than 30-40 minutes! Its tiny CPU gets hot and can damage the device itself.

How to Update A-OBD

Software Requirements

1. ESP Flasher Tool:

Windows (.zip) macOS / Linux

2. The .bin firmware file for your version:

C3 V5 WROOM/S1 V5
Update Steps

1. Download the ESP Flasher Tool and the correct .bin file for your version (C3 or WROOM).

2. Connect your A-OBD to the PC/laptop with a USB data cable.

3. Open ESP Flasher and select the COM port your A-OBD is connected to.

4. Select the downloaded .bin file and start flashing. Wait for it to complete.

5. Unplug and reconnect the device. Connect to the A-OBD WiFi and open 192.168.4.1.

⚠️ Flash the wrong version's file and the device won't work — double-check C3 vs WROOM.

Watch: Flashing Walkthrough Flashing Fails? Bootloader Mode

If the flasher can't connect, force the board into bootloader mode first:

ESP32-C3 SuperMini: connect the board to your PC via USB, then press and hold the BOOT button, press and release RST while still holding BOOT, then release BOOT.

ESP32/WROOM (38-pin): connect the board to your PC via USB, then press and hold the BOOT (IO0) button, press and release EN while still holding BOOT, then release BOOT.

Then select the COM port again in ESP Flasher and retry. Still stuck? Check you're using a USB data cable (not charge-only) and try a different USB port.
